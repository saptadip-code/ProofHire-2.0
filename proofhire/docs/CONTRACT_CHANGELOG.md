2026-10-11 | M3 Backend | CLARIFICATION: `PATCH /assessments/{id}/checklist/{item_id}` checks in this order: unknown assessment 404, caller not the owner 403 (JWT user or matching `X-Owner-Token`), item missing or part of another assessment 404. 2026-10-11 | M3 Backend | CLARIFICATION: `POST /assessments/compare` rows are, in this fixed order: `risk_score`, `risk_level`, `upfront_fee_amount`, `salary_monthly`, `email_type`, `website_present`, `interview_mode`, `application_channel`, `observed_risk_signals`, `approved_reports`, `connections_count`. 2026-10-11 | M3 Backend | CLARIFICATION: in compare, `better` means "lower risk wins": lower score, lower level (low<medium<high), lower fee, company e-mail over free/no e-mail, website present, a structured interview (video_call / in_person / phone_call) over chat_only / none, no WhatsApp/Telegram-only channel, fewer observed risk signals, fewer approved reports, fewer connections. `salary_monthly` is always `tie`. Compare is public and never returns contact e-mail or phone. | M2 Frontend
